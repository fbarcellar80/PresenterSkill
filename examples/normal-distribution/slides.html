The empirical rule

Three numbers describe almost every bell curve

68.3%within 1σ of the mean
95.4%within 2σ
99.7%within 3σ

σ ("sigma") is the standard deviation. Next, you will move the curve yourself.

Applying it

A z-score tells you how unusual a value is

z = (x − μ) / σ

Positive z: above average. Negative z: below. |z| > 2 is unusual; |z| > 3 is rare. See the bands
